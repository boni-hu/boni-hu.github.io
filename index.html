About Me

Hi! I am a final-year PhD student, fortunately under the supervision of Prof. Shuhui Bu. Previously, I am lucky to have opportunities to work at the UTS Robotics Institude as a visiting student, supervised by Liang Zhao and Shoudong Huang. My research focuses on place recognition, geolocalization and relocalization in remote sensing, with emphasis on vision representations that integrate geometric structures and semantic relationships to enable accurate and robust spatial understanding from aerial perspectives. Currently, my exploration focuses on equipping large language models and vision-language models (VLMs) with spatial intelligence for remote sensing applications. I also have a strong interest in applying these techniques to medical imaging and surgical robotics applications.

[TOP] I am actively seeking postdoctoral and industry opportunities starting from 2026.
