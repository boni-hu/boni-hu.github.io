Biography

I am a 4th-year PhD student, advised by Prof. Shuhui Bu.My research focuses on vision representation for remote sensing in place recognition and geolocalization, with applications in UAV platforms. I am deeply interested in exploring elegant vision representations, particularly for unmanned and medical devices.
