Biography

I am a fourth-year PhD candidate under the supervision of Prof. Shuhui Bu. My research centers on developing advanced vision representations for remote sensing applications, with a particular emphasis on place recognition and geolocalization systems deployed on UAV platforms. I am passionate about designing elegant and efficient visual representation methods. Looking ahead, I am excited to explore the integration of spatial intelligence into large language models and vision-language models (VLMs).
